# Event image stock pictures

`src/constants/samples.ts`, keys `sample-1` … `sample-6`). No people in them
(the user, 2026-11-21). 3:4 centre crops, 750x1000 (1 is 600x800). All from
Unsplash under the Unsplash License. The same files are in
`web/public/shoot/sample-N.jpg` and `targets/clip/Samples/sample-N.jpg`.
