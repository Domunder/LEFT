# Keyword backlog
Researched 2026-11-21 (free sources: web search result sets for ~50 queries, the question/"people also ask"-style phrasing they surface, WeddingWire / The Knot / Mumsnet forum threads, and competitor blogs — pov.camera, GuestCam, WedUploader, Lense, Yogile, knipsmig, Goosechase, Scavify). No volume data; demand is an evidence-based estimate.

Limits of this research: the search tool is US-only and is not Google's own results page, so "SERP" means "who showed up for the query", not exact positions. Reddit threads did not surface in any query (forum evidence is WeddingWire, The Knot and Mumsnet instead). Every row's SERP rating rests on the result set of one or two queries (rows 1–3, 7, 8 and 10 on two or more); none was checked in Google itself, so re-check the top results before writing each post.

## Clusters
- **Real disposable cameras** — people about to buy film cameras for an event are the closest thing to a LEFT buyer; they search for counts, costs and whether it works.
- **Guest photos at weddings** — how couples get, collect and use what guests shoot; the core job LEFT does.
- **Unplugged and phone etiquette** — couples who want guests present, not on their screens; LEFT's hidden-until-developed roll speaks to the same wish.
- **Photo challenges and games** — scavenger hunts, dares and icebreakers; the search-side name for missions.
- **Parties and pre-wedding events** — bachelorette, birthdays, NYE, showers; the free tier's natural home and a route to the bride before the wedding.
- **Group trips** — sharing and shooting a trip as a group; second use case, strongest in spring and summer.
- **Film look and photo craft** — how to get the disposable/flash look; top-of-funnel traffic that the four looks answer honestly.
