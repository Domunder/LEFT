# Trips — `left.roadtrip`

**Bio:** Friend trips on film. See the photos when you're home · by LEFT 📷
**Link:** leftmoment.com (`?utm_source={platform}&utm_campaign=left.roadtrip`)
**Looks used:** Golden for summer, Chrome for mountains and the sea, Mono for cities.

## Audience

20–30: friend trips, gap years, interrail, ski weeks, festivals abroad,
bachelor and bachelorette trips. They want the trip to feel like a story and
end up with one shared album instead of 6 camera rolls.

## Voice

Dreamy, nostalgic, a bit wistful. Slow. Let the photos breathe — few words per
slide. Humour is gentle.
