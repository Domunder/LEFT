# Weddings — `left.weddings`

**Bio:** Wedding ideas for guest photos & film lovers · by LEFT 📷
**Link:** leftmoment.com/wedding (`?utm_source={platform}&utm_campaign=left.weddings`)
**Looks used:** mostly Golden and Mono, Faded for vintage themes.

## Audience

Couples 25–35 planning a wedding (6–12 months out), maids of honour, best men,
wedding planners. They save and share ideas. On Instagram they save carousels;
on TikTok they search "wedding ideas", "unplugged wedding", "wedding guest
photos".

## Voice

A friend who has been to too many weddings and has good ideas. Warm, a bit
witty, never cynical about marriage. Lowercase hooks on TikTok are fine.
