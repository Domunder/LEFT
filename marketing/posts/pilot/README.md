# Pilot — posting list

Two accounts, two strategies, 7 days (STRATEGY.md → The pilot). One post per
account per day, the same slides on TikTok and Instagram. Finished pictures
and the caption to paste are in `marketing/queue/pilot/{id}/` (`tiktok/`,
`instagram/`, `caption.txt`) — made by `cd web && npm run social`.

Post between 18:00 and 21:00 (weekend: 11:00–13:00). Pick the sound in the
app. 48 hours after each post, screenshot its insights.

| Day | `left.weddings` — story slideshows | `left.afterdark` — one picture, one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `weddings-1` morning after · "312 photos you have never seen" | `afterdark-1` "3 shots left and it's only 11pm" |
| 2 | `weddings-2` unknown photo · "one you don't know exists yet" | `afterdark-2` "dance accordingly" |
| 3 | `weddings-3` mission · "a secret photo mission" | `afterdark-3` "the photo you won't remember taking" |
| 4 | `weddings-4` **control**: what LEFT is, app screens | `afterdark-4` "birthday idea: 10 shots each" |
| 5 | `weddings-5` morning after, hook 2 · "until the next morning" | `afterdark-5` "it's 2004 and you're waiting for your film" |
| 6 | `weddings-6` unknown photo, hook 2 · "80 places at once" | `afterdark-6` "uses all 10 shots in the first hour" |
| 7 | `weddings-7` mission, hook 2 · "table 3 talking to table 9" | `afterdark-7` "the trip you couldn't see" (trip test) |

Wedding photos are one event per post from `stock/wedding/` (couple-6 the night, couple-3 the beach, general the missions); party photos are the website's. All Unsplash, logged in `stock/LICENSES.md`, graded
with LEFT's looks. The people in them are strangers: the texts are ideas and
POVs, never something that happened to them.
