# Outreach messages

The `prospect-finder` agent writes every draft from this file. The user edits
this file to change the voice — not the agent.

## Sender

- **Name:** Dominik
- **Who:** the person who built LEFT.
- **Offer before launch:** LEFT free for their event, in return for honest
  feedback afterwards. (Free up to 50 guests anyway; for bigger events the
  user decides case by case — the agent never promises a free paid tier.)

## Voice

- 2–4 sentences. Reads like a person, not a brand. No links in the first
  message (spam filters and trust) — the link comes when they reply.
- Opens with the **one specific thing** from their post (date, venue type,
  theme, a worry they wrote about). Never "I came across your profile".
- Says plainly what LEFT is and that the sender built it.
- Ends with an easy question, never pressure. No emojis beyond one.
- In the language of their post (English or German). Du in German.
- Never mention their followers, looks, or anything not about the event.

## What LEFT is, in one line (pick one)

- "an app where every guest gets a small roll of shots, like a disposable
  camera, and nobody sees the photos until after the event"
- "a disposable camera in every guest's phone: 10 shots each, and all the
  photos are developed together the morning after"

## Templates — adapt, never send unchanged

**Wedding**
> Congrats on the engagement! Saw you're planning for {month} — {their
> detail}. I built a little app for exactly this: every guest gets a small roll
> of shots like a disposable camera, and the photos only reveal the morning
> after. Would you want to try it at your wedding? It's free, I'd just love
> your honest feedback.

**Birthday / party**
> {their detail} sounds like a great night! I built an app that turns every
> guest's phone into a disposable camera — 10 shots each, and nobody sees the
> photos until the next morning. Want to try it for your {event}? Free, I'm
> just looking for honest feedback.

**Trip**
> {their detail} — that trip sounds amazing. I built an app where everyone in
> the group gets a roll of shots and the photos only develop when the trip is
> over. Want to try it? It's free, just looking for feedback.

**Someone who asked for ideas (Reddit, forums)**
> Saw your post asking about {their question}. Not sure if it fits, but I
> built an app for this: guests get a small roll of shots each and the photos
> reveal after the event, all in one album. Happy to set it up for you for
> free if you want to try it.

**German (Hochzeit)**

## When they reply

- **Interested:** send leftmoment.com and offer to set it up together on a
  call or in chat. Note the event date in `log.md`.
- **No / not now:** thank them, mark them `declined`, never message again.
- **Questions about price, privacy, guests needing the app:** answer honestly
  from `marketing/CLAUDE.md` → product facts. Guests do install the app.
